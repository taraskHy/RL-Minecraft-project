Rules for the night

  • At most three training processes on the GPU at once; more spills into system memory and slows everything 5 to 10 times.
  • Every number says where it was measured: simulator, offline bot (Mineflayer's physics without a server) or real Minecraft.
  • Visuals never change the physics or the policy. Colourful blocks in Minecraft are full cubes with normal friction only (concrete, quartz, gold, emerald), so the bot moves exactly as on stone.
  • The current final policy stays available whatever happens.
